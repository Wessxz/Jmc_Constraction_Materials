2. Delivery Policy

We currently deliver within Muntinlupa City only.

  • Delivery is FREE for all orders within Muntinlupa City.
  • Orders outside Muntinlupa City are not eligible for delivery at this time.
  • Delivery is done using our own trucks. Orders will be scheduled for delivery once the truck load is sufficiently filled.
  • Delivery schedules may vary depending on the volume of orders. We will notify you once your order is ready for delivery.
  • A valid address within Muntinlupa City must be provided at the time of order placement.

3. Payment

  • We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) only.
  • Payment is collected upon delivery of your order.
  • Please prepare the exact amount or close to the total order amount to facilitate smooth transactions.
  • Receipts will be provided upon payment.

4. Order Cancellation

  • You may cancel your order at any time as long as the products have not yet been loaded onto the delivery truck.
  • Once your order is marked as "Out for Delivery" or has been loaded for dispatch, cancellation is no longer possible.
  • To cancel an order, contact us immediately through our store or send us a message via the contact details provided below.

5. Returns and Refunds

  • Returns are accepted within 7 days from the date of delivery.
  • Items must be in their original condition — unused and undamaged.
  • Returns are subject to inspection by our staff before approval.
  • Once approved, a refund or product replacement will be arranged at our discretion.
  • Returns outside the 7-day period will not be accepted.
  • To initiate a return, bring the item to our store or contact us at the details below with your order reference number.

6. Order Quantities and Truck Load

  • There is no minimum order amount.
  • However, all orders are subject to available truck capacity. Orders will be grouped and delivered once the truck has sufficient load.
  • Large orders that exceed a single truck's capacity may be split into multiple deliveries. We will inform you of any such arrangements.

  • All products are subject to availability. Stocks displayed on the website are updated regularly but may not always reflect real-time inventory.
